Drug Databases

    Database of bioactive molecules with drug-like properties, biological activities, and molecular targets.

    Chemical structures, physicochemical properties, bioassays, and compound information.

    Visual summaries of top-selling drugs, their chemical structures, and other medicinal chemistry information.

Scientific Literature and Databases

Literature Search Engines

    Biomedical and life sciences literature database, including full-text articles and preprints.

Scientific Visualization and Illustration

Scientific Illustration and Graphics

  • Inkscape
    A professional, free, and open-source vector graphics editor.
  • SilicoDraw
    A web-based scientific illustration and diagramming tool for creating publication-quality scientific figures.

Molecular Graphics

Computational Chemistry and Molecular Modeling

Molecular Visualization

  • UCSF ChimeraX
    Molecular visualization and structural analysis software for proteins, macromolecular complexes, and experimental structural data.
  • PyMOL
    Molecular visualization software for rendering, analyzing, and creating publication-quality representations of molecular structures.
  • Visual Molecular Dynamics (VMD)
    Molecular visualization and analysis software, particularly useful for biomolecular systems and molecular dynamics trajectories.

Molecular Docking

  • AutoDock Vina
    Open-source molecular docking software for predicting protein–ligand binding poses and performing virtual screening.
  • AutoDock-GPU
    GPU-accelerated molecular docking software for high-throughput virtual screening.

ADMET Prediction

  • SwissADME
    Prediction of physicochemical properties, drug-likeness, pharmacokinetic parameters, and medicinal chemistry friendliness of small molecules.
  • pkCSM
    Prediction of pharmacokinetic and toxicity properties using graph-based signatures.
  • ADMETlab 3.0
    Comprehensive web-based platform for predicting absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, and toxicity properties.
  • ProTox 3.0
    Web-based platform for predicting chemical toxicity and toxicological endpoints.

Technical Tools and Productivity

  • Obsidian
    A knowledge base that works on top of a local folder of plain-text Markdown files. Excellent for connecting ideas.
